Overview

Profundum is a mass bass instrument. Instead of stacking layers it plays one bass voice and lets you choose how heavy that voice is: a pure sub, a resonant body, a harmonic growl or a driven, folded bite. Wherever the sound goes, the low end stays centred.

Every voice has two parts. The Mass is the harmonic body of the note — its depth, body and edge, its fold, its character and its weight. The Anchor is a separate sub tone underneath it, identical in both channels, that holds the fundamental in place however hard the Mass is driven, folded or widened.

Every sound parameter holds two values, endpoint A and endpoint B. Bass Morph moves the sound from one to the other, so a single control can carry a clean sub into a distorted growl along a path designed at both ends. The Mass control and four personality macros — Weight, Dark/Bright, Tight/Loose and Clean/Violent — steer the sound on top, and Motion moves it over time.

Features

  • Harmonic core — Depth, Body, Edge and Spectral Shift shape the harmonic recipe of the note, with an excitation stage, Shape, Harmonic Fold and six Character types (Tube, Wavefold, Rectify, Bite, Crush, Fracture)
  • Sub anchor — Five waveforms and four modes (Lock, Follow, Punch, Ghost); the Anchor is never widened and reaches the fold only through Anchor Fold Feed
  • Mass — One control moves depth, body, transient, fold, compression and anchor level together, level-trimmed so heavier does not just mean louder
  • Bass Morph — Two named endpoints, an Edit Target, Compare A / A·B / B, Copy A → B and B → A, and three Anchor morph behaviours (Hold, Blend, Yield)
  • Four personality macros — Weight, Dark/Bright, Tight/Loose and Clean/Violent, neutral at the centre, drawn as offset rings on the knobs they reach
  • Pitch and envelopes — A pitch Drop with shape and rebound, an amp envelope with curves, Mono priority, legato, glide and Note Memory
  • Mono-safe stereo field — Solid, Spread and Wide image modes with a crossover below which the bass stays centred
  • Motion — An Env, Cycle or Drift source aimed at Mass, Depth, Body, Edge or Shift
  • Live harmonic display — The measured harmonics of the sound you hear, with the Anchor in its own lane
  • 53 factory presets — Six categories, every preset generated from the plugin’s own parameter surface and verified to make its named capability audible
  • Real-time safe — process() allocates nothing, takes no locks, throws nothing, and does no I/O
  • Cross-platform — VST3 (Windows, macOS, Linux) + Audio Unit (macOS)

Factory Presets

53 factory presets ship with the plugin in six categories. Each one is built around one capability of the instrument and names it in its comment, and each is verified by a render to make that capability audible and to keep its low end intact in a mono sum.

  • Sub — Plain, heavy floors: the Anchor waveforms, Lock, Mass and long tails
  • Resonant — Resonant bodies played as lines: glide, legato, swells and morphs
  • Harmonic — Harmonic bodies, widened images and the full morph span
  • Driven — The six Character types, Harmonic Fold and the Anchor fed into the fold
  • Plucked — Pitch drops, punches, ghost thumps and hard attacks
  • Animated — Motion sources sweeping Mass, Depth, Body, Edge and Shift, and moving width

System Requirements

Windows

Windows 10 or later, 64-bit, a VST3 host.

macOS

macOS 12 or later, a VST3 or Audio Unit host.

Linux

A 64-bit distribution with a VST3 host.

Installation

Windows

Run the .exe installer. The plugin will be installed to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Krate Audio\Profundum\Profundum.vst3

macOS

Double-click the .pkg installer. Both formats will be installed:

/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Profundum.vst3
/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Profundum.component

Linux

Extract the archive and copy to your VST3 folder:

cp -r Profundum.vst3 ~/.vst3/
