verdicts rather than only in per-task notes.

  later tasks not to start T006 assuming a budget-compliant header exists, and not to lower `kMaxLoops`, raise
  the budget, or relax a threshold to force a pass. This is the direct ancestor of the current FR-080/SC-004
  failures — the gap was known and flagged before implementation began, and remains unresolved at the end of
  the phase.

  measurement showed budget was missed, and no measurement existed yet at that task); a later pass (reflected

  documented in FR-080's evidence as architectural (six `CrossfadingDelayLine`s' cache-unfriendly access

  record the exact `normaliseRows()` arithmetic that explains why coupling regeneration ends up *smaller*, not
  larger, at high coupling. This is now the confirmed, measured, unresolved SC-002 failure above.
