Membrum kit-quality probes (membrum-audit, 2026-11-23). Source: specs/membrum-audit/probes/audit_quality_probes.cpp.txt
Harness: real Membrum::Processor, 48 kHz, block 256, 16 stereo output buses wired (aux active only for the output-bus probe).
Kits loaded from resources/presets/Kit Presets/<Category>/<name>.vstpreset (component chunk -> Processor::setState); between
hits setActive(false) / setState(kit) / setActive(true) so every hit starts from silence with a reset bus limiter.
Parameter sweeps: a fresh Processor (default kit) per render; context and the probed value sent through IParameterChanges in
the note-on block. Note-on velocity 100/127 unless stated. Renders stop early once no voice is active and 16 blocks are < 1e-8.

FEATURES (mono = (L+R)/2; the pan probe uses L):
  peak dBFS       max |L|,|R| over the render
  rms500 dBFS     RMS of mono over the first 500 ms
  dc              mean of mono over the whole render (flag |dc| > 1e-3 = -60 dBFS)
  nonFinite       non-finite samples in L and R
  decay           10 ms RMS frames; time until the envelope stays 60 dB under its maximum; ">cap" if still above at the end
                  (cap 4 s kit table, 3 s parameter sweeps)
  centroid Hz     magnitude-weighted spectral centroid, first 200 ms (Hann 4096, hop 1024, zero-padded edge frames, DC skipped)
  bands           24 log-spaced bands 40 Hz..16 kHz, power averaged over the first 1 s (Hann 8192, hop 2048),
                  dB normalised to the loudest band, floored at -80 dB
  bandDist(a,b)   RMS over the 24 bands of (a - b) in dB
KIT FLAGS: silent = enabled and peak < -60 dBFS; limiter = peak - (kit master dB - 1) >= -0.5 dB; dc; nan; long ring = decay > 4 s.
SAMENESS: near-duplicate = bandDist < 3 dB AND centroid ratio <= 1.10 AND decay ratio <= 1.20 (silent pads excluded).
VELOCITY: vel 40/127 vs 127/127, 1 s render; flag rms500 diff < 6 dB AND centroid change < 5 %.
PARAMETERS: steps 0/.25/.5/.75/1 (discrete: every step); per base pad: level = max-min rms500 dB (floored at -100),
  centroid % = max/min-1, decay % = max/min-1 (non-silent steps), bandDist = max pairwise, bit-identical over the whole range;
  bad = bit-identical on all 3 base pads; suspicious = every metric < 1 dB / 5 % / 5 % / 1 dB on all 3, or (continuous only)
  an adjacent-step rms500 jump > 12 dB between two non-silent steps. live = changing the value (to 0 and to 1) at 299 ms while
  tom 41 rings (same context) alters the output after the change.
CSV: kit-pads.csv (every pad of every kit), kit-pairs.csv (every within-kit pair), param-audibility.csv (param x base pad).
