5. Classification (what the code says)
   - Latched at the control latch (offset 0 of the next block, or at a note-on), as every Controls field is.
   - punchTimeMs, ghostTimeMs: consumed ONLY when an arm program is built, i.e. at the next re-arming
     note-on (snapArm :1011-1027 / bridgedArm :1030-1058, called from applyNoteOn :505, :514). A change
     while a program runs does not alter it.
   - punchBoostDb: consumed when an arm program is built (boostGain at :1018, :1038) and additionally as
     the range cap of the Hermite bridge-length fit (bridgeLength :994-995) whenever a bridge is built,
     including the FR-044 mode bridge (bridgeToRest from latchControls :445-446). It never steps a running
     program's gain.
   - Net: none of the three is immediate; a per-interval morph of them reaches the audio at the next
     re-arming note-on (and, for punchBoostDb, only as a bridge-length bound at a mode bridge).
