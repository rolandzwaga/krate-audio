STOP AND SURFACE - SC-003(a) and SC-022 are unreachable as written (rule 13):
  SC-003(a) asks |dp| <= dpmax * (1 + 4*FLT_EPSILON) per interval. SlewLimiter::process (smoother.h:549) computes
  current_ += riseRate_ in float, so each step is fl(p + r) - p, which differs from r by up to 0.5 ulp(p) ABSOLUTE
  (2^-25 for p in [0.5, 1)). The spec's tolerance covers only the float computation of r (calculateSlewRate), not the
  rounding of the addition; relative to a small r the absolute 0.5 ulp dominates. The plan mandates the SlewLimiter
  (FR-020, plan S4.2), so the engine cannot avoid it without a plan / spec change.
  Emulated worst (max(|dp|, |dp_a|) / dpmax - 1) / FLT_EPSILON over the three jumps (allowed: 4.0):
