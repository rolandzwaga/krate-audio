DEVIATION (case 4, eSubDb expected value - surface it):
  tasks.md T007 case 4 says eSubDb of a sine of amplitude a "equals 20*log10(a/sqrt2) within 0.05 dB". The reader
  is the spec's (mean of MassDynamicsTest::subBand(...).lpRmsDb, spec descriptor block E_sub; plan S5.7), and
  subBand's level is the RMS of a 4th-order Butterworth low-pass at 1.5*f0 (mass_dynamics_test_helpers.h:604-608,
  biquad.h:427-436 setButterworth, RBJ bilinear with prewarp). That filter passes f0 at |H| = 1/sqrt(1 + (2/3)^8)
  = -0.166 dB, so the reader as specified reads 20*log10(a/sqrt2) - 0.166 dB on a pure sine: the bar as written
  cannot hold (0.166 > 0.05) for any correct implementation of the specified reader. The test keeps the reader
  exactly as specified and the 0.05 dB tolerance, and puts the filter's analytic gain into the expected value:
  expected = 20*log10(a/sqrt2) + 20*log10|H(f0)|, |H| the prewarped analog Butterworth magnitude
  1/sqrt(1 + Omega^8), Omega = tan(pi f0/fs) / tan(pi fc/fs), fc = float(1.5 f0). No tolerance is relaxed and no
  gate reads absolute E_sub (SC-005(a), SC-008(d), SC-015 compare E_sub against E_sub, where the constant cancels).
  If the main loop prefers the reader to compensate |H(f0)| instead, that is a one-line change in eSubDb and the
  test's expected value returns to 20*log10(a/sqrt2).
