1. SC-012(e) input gating (C-3) is per (fixture, note): the Rectify input does not depend on Amount / Tone.
   Matching rule (C-2): unambiguous output -> perceivedPitchRatio()*f0 within 1 cent of f_hat; ambiguous ->
   within 1 cent of one of the two best of {f_hat/2, f_hat, 2 f_hat}. Caps (<= 20 % ambiguous, >= 8
   unambiguous over the 33 Amount x Tone cells) apply to gated (fixture, note) in the [long] case only (the
   smoke has 2 cells). Ungated inputs are WARN-recorded with their counts. The premise "w2 within 40 dB of
   w1" is read as 10*log10(w2/w1) >= -40 at the Rectify input (even harmonics present; w2 above w1 also
   satisfies it). The smoke additionally CHECKs that the kSawAnchor E1 chain input is gated (otherwise the
   smoke would assert nothing).

6. SC-025 Drop: f[n] = E2*2^(d[n]/12) from the task's DropEnvelope settings, preceded by a 24576-sample
   (0.512 s, 48 x 512, 1536 x 16) pre-roll held at d = 48 st so every stage starts the Drop settled; the core
   receives f as f0PerSample from note on; the stages receive the staircase stair[n] = f[n - n % 16].
7. SC-025(a) frame: a BH7 frame of 8 cycles of the core's instantaneous phase (Phi = cumulative stair/fs),
   window as a function of Phi, samples weighted by dPhi (see the TU banner). A time-domain 8-period frame
   spans most of the 48-semitone chirp and smears every harmonic across its neighbours' bins, so a correct
   stage would fail it; the phase-domain frame is the BH7 analysis of harmonic n of the instantaneous f0 and
   reduces exactly to the time-domain BH7 frame on the steady render. Instants: the first samples after the
   pre-roll where d <= {24, 12, 6, 3, 1.5} st (frame starts); the instant's f0 = the staircase f at the
   frame's phase midpoint; the steady render is the core noteOn(f0) + the stage keyed to f0, frame after
   1 s. Harmonics n <= 16 with n*f0 < 0.45 fs; a harmonic whose input is more than 60 dB below the frame's
   strongest input harmonic is not compared, and output levels are floored at -60 dB re the strongest output
   harmonic (OQ-S floor convention); at least 8 harmonics must be compared (non-vacuity guard).
8. SC-025(b): the stage fed setFundamentalHz(stair[block start]) per 512 block, no pointer; ShapeMorph cells
   and Crush at Tone 0.5; gated as "largest |dH| over every such cell, fixture and instant > 1.5 dB" (the row
