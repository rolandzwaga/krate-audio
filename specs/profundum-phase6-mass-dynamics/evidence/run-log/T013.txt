   no makeup) over the window. GR is independent of the makeup (detection is pre-gain), so the harvest's makeup choice
   does not feed back into its own GR readings.
2. The Mass reference voice / S8.3 calibration voice = ChainConfig defaults (kDefaultControls base, Anchor attached,
   law base depth/body from base and Targets{} for the rest, shaper +6 / 0) with massAt = 50 and

   both subBand readings of outL over samples [48000, 144000); the LP group delay is printed. (c) runs at E0 only

   [1 s, 2 s) and high [3 s, 4 s); amount-1 steady GR on both segments printed (recorded only).
6. SC-024(d): renderPoly 0.5 s, both orders; every cornerHzPerInterval entry whose interval ends at or after 200 ms
   (sample 9600, entry 299 on) is within 1 % of 2*E1; the discrimination CHECKs both the analytic separation of the
   two rules (> 1 %) and that no settled measured corner falls inside the 1 % window of 2*C3.
7. Harvest, plan S8.3, in this order:
   (1) bisect the row-4 (amount 1) threshold over [-60, 0] dB on min over {E0, E1, E2, C3} of the calibration GR to
       4.0 +/- 0.05 dB (40-iteration cap; not reaching 3.95 dB at -60 dB, or no convergence -> STOP AND SURFACE target1).
   (2) a100 = MassLaw::evaluate(100, Targets{}).compressionAmount; r = the first row with amount >= a100. If
       min(GR100 E1, GR100 C3) < 3.5 dB, bisect row r's threshold over [-60, current] (to 0.01 dB, keeping the highest
       threshold that meets 3.5), rows above r clamped to <= row r (monotone). Unreachable at -60 -> STOP target2.
   (3) "Intermediate thresholds linear in amount between harvested rows": the harvested rows are row 0 (amount 0, its
       seed threshold 0 dB; slope 0 so it never compresses), row 4 and row r. Every other row's threshold is the linear
       interpolation in amount between its nearest harvested neighbours. NOTE for the main loop: with r = 4 (C-7 (i)) the
       rows 1-3 interpolate from 0 dB at amount 0, so the low rows sit well above the seed's -12 / -16 / -19 dB and may

       text, recorded here so it can be ruled on if a different lower anchor is wanted (one change in the interpolate
       lambda's harvested[] mask). GR per row is CHECKed monotone non-decreasing in amount at every note (1e-6 dB float
       allowance for rows reading ~0 on both sides).
   (4) makeupDb (rows 1-4) = mean of the E1 and C3 steady GR; row 0 makeup 0; spread |E1 - C3| > 0.8 dB -> STOP target3.
       The header's static_assert invariants (threshold non-increasing, makeup non-decreasing) are checked on the result;
