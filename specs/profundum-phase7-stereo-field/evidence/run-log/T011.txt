## Decisions (recorded; surface, do not silently revert)
- Representatives: the 64 per-partial series of a cell share one m(t). For Cycle the TU gates the affine identity
  p_k = s_k P_k (1 - 0.5 mu + 0.5 mu m(t)) within 1e-6 on every moving partial (the slew never engages: the fastest Cycle,
  1 Hz at 0.5 mu P <= 0.5, moves <= 0.0021 per interval < 0.003), so the spectral peak and the 1/e fit are read on two
  representatives (largest and smallest moving P_k) rather than 62 FFTs per cell. Drift's affine deviation is printed.
- Cycle inward-motion mean: over the largest whole number of Cycle periods inside the render (a part period biases the mean
  of a 0.2236 Hz sine by up to ~2.4 % of 0.5 mu P_k, above the 1e-3 bar); the 60 s mean is printed (inward line, CSV).
  Tolerance read as 1e-3 * P_k (the spec's "within 1e-3 of P_k"), tighter than tasks.md's absolute 1e-3 since P_k <= 1.
- BH7 peak: the window-weighted mean is removed before windowing (p_k(t) sits on p-bar). A Node model of a pure sine at
  0.05, 0.2236, 0.75, 0.625 and 1.0 Hz (90 000 samples at 1500 Hz, 2^17 FFT) puts the peak on the nearest bin in each case
  (offsets -0.37 .. +0.46 bin; 1.0 Hz -> 0.99564 Hz <= 1 Hz).
- No-sign-change: p == 0 (centre, reached when m = -1 at mu = 1) is allowed and counted (zero_hits); any other sample must
  carry s_k's sign bit (spec FR-030: "sign(p_k(t)) is the same s_k at every t where p_k(t) != 0").
- The synced cells use rate 0.3 (ignored while synced) and mu 1, Spread 1.
- Drop: the reference f0 is pitchHoldAlignedF0 of the core's float track, so the held reference is phase-aligned with the
  core for the click count; the residual (held - interpolated) shares the oscillators and does not depend on it. Clicks are
  counted from 0.25 s (past the note-on ramp, before the glide at 0.5 s); the window N uses f0 = C3 (the strictest N).
- Deviation (Smoke): tasks.md says "arm (1) at 2 s"; a 2 s render (96 000 samples) cannot hold sidebandPower's 2^17-sample
  frame (2.73 s at 48 kHz), so the Smoke's arm (1) is the 0 -> 1 step at 1 s in a 3.5 s render (frame from 0.75 s); the
  validity arm runs at 2 s.
