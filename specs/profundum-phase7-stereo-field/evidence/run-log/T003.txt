## Decisions
1. DAZ-immune range handling (not in the plan text; forced by dsp_test_main's FTZ/DAZ): the IsValidControls
   case asks that nextafter(0, -1) (a negative DENORMAL) be invalid. Under DAZ it compares equal to 0, so
   isValidControls rejects any negative non-zero bit pattern before the range compare (every range has lo >= 0),
   and setControls clamps with `v <= lo -> lo` / `v >= hi -> hi`, which also maps -0 and negative denormals to +0
   (rule 4: zero is +0).
2. m_ = isFinite(raw) ? clamp(raw, -1, 1) : 0. The sources are finite and in range by construction (Drift clamps,
   LFO Sine table); the guard makes FR-034's [-1, 1] hold by construction and never hands a non-finite to clamp.
3. The tempo is latched like the controls: setTempoBpm stores it, advanceControl (via the change cache) passes
   bpm_eff to the LFO. cycleFrequencyHz() is computed with the LFO's own float expression (bpm_eff / 60) / beats,
   so it equals the rate the LFO is set to. The fold loop is under `if constexpr (kCycleSyncFold)` (no
   unreachable-code path when the switch is flipped).
4. The free Cycle frequency is always forwarded to lfo_.setFrequency on a rate change, even while synced
   (LFO::setFrequency does not touch the increment while synced), so a sync -> free switch resumes at the right rate.
5. reset() re-runs applyMotionConfig() before drift_.reset() / lfo_.reset(), because reset latches pending_
   (S3 step 1) and the rate / sync may have changed since the last interval.
6. reset() calls crossover_.setCrossoverFrequency(2 f_c) always and crossover_.prepare(sampleRate_) only if
   prepared_ (C-4 as tasks.md words it).
7. The constructor sets the stage crossover's smoothing / tracking / target, snaps the side gain and mode ramp,
   applies the MOTION config and fills the law cache, so every observer is defined before prepare()
   (LifecycleBeforePrepare).
8. Controls members are declared without `{}` (default-initialised through the NSDMIs) to avoid clang's
   "default member initializer needed within definition of enclosing class" hazard.
9. SideGainLaw additionally checks deviation 5 through the probe: the members start at the constants and reset()
   leaves probe-set values alone. Small addition, in scope (deviation 5 is part of T003).
